# showsAsPrimaryButton

**Instance Property**

Whether to prioritize Apple Pay by showing it prominently at the top.
When <code>true</code> (default), Apple Pay is displayed as a separate button at the top.
When <code>false</code>, Apple Pay is grouped with other payment methods:
